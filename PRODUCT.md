# Our Little Arcade — Stage 2

Private two-person couples app. Stage 1's pink keepsake is approved. Stage 2 adds private account/couple handling, tab destinations, Tic-Tac-Toe, Connect Four and the scoreboard. Memories and Notes are explicitly Stage 4 placeholders. Mood/nudges are Stage 3; wishlists/photos/notes Stage 4; packaging Stage 5; remaining games Stage 6.

No personal names, preferences or anniversary date are baked into the active UI. The date is entered in setup and editable in Us; nicknames are optional. Existing database data is preserved. Night Train stays opt-in. No paid services, analytics or advertising. Online board play uses server validation and private Realtime, but hosted setup and two-device acceptance remain owner-dependent. Preview board games are pass-and-play and clear on reload/sign-out. No APK/PWA/live deployment claim is made.
