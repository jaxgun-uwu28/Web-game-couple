# Wishes, Memories and Notes

Operate within the approved pink keepsake world. Us keeps personal settings and grows into wishlists: named list tabs, a warm wish jar, generous paper rows and a thumb-reachable wish sheet. Memories is a photographic timeline led by the users' real photos; its initial state is an invitation to save a moment. Notes is a stack of peach envelopes and a white handwritten reading page. Keep existing fonts, tokens and 44px controls. No new identity or stock photography.

Private states are part of the interaction: secret lists never reach the other account, gift claims appear only to the claimer, letters reveal only through the validated server function, and daily photos open after both post. Signed image links expire. Empty/loading/error/busy states explain recovery. Forms keep their input if save fails. Mobile forms use a bottom sheet with keyboard and safe-area space. Preview is explicitly local and switches accounts for privacy demonstrations.
